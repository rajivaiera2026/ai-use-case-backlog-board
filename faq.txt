FIRSTCUT AI - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
========================================
NOTE: This is demo content. FirstCut AI is a fictional studio; all names,
prices, contact details and examples below are illustrative and should be
replaced with real business data before publishing.

We help seed to Series A product teams in Bengaluru find, choose and ship
their first AI feature - the one that moves a metric, not the one that looks
good in a deck.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT DOES FIRSTCUT AI ACTUALLY DO?
We run short, fixed-scope engagements that take you from "we should do
something with AI" to one AI feature live in your product, measured and
owned by your team. Discovery, prototype, production build, evaluation and
handover - one engagement, one team.

2. WHO DO YOU WORK WITH?
Seed to Series A product teams (roughly 5-60 people) based in Bengaluru and
across India. You have a real product, real users, and a product owner who
can make decisions in days, not months. We are a fit if you can start
within 2-3 weeks.

3. WE DON'T KNOW WHICH AI FEATURE TO BUILD FIRST. CAN YOU HELP?
That is the core of the engagement. Week 1 is a feature-selection sprint:
we score candidate ideas against user pain, data readiness, model
feasibility, cost per call and time-to-ship, then recommend one to build.
You get the written reasoning even if you stop there.

4. HOW LONG DOES IT TAKE?
4 weeks for a FirstCut Sprint (1 week discovery + 3 weeks build). A typical
engagement ends up 5-7 weeks including evaluation and handover. Your
kickoff happens within 10 working days of signing.

5. HOW MUCH DOES IT COST?
FirstCut Sprint (4 weeks): Rs 4,50,000 - Rs 7,50,000, fixed before kickoff.
Price depends on scope, number of integrations, and evaluation complexity.
Discovery-only (1 week): Rs 1,25,000, fully credited against a Sprint
started within 60 days.
Ongoing retainers: Rs 3,50,000 - Rs 6,00,000 per month.
Model and API usage (e.g. LLM, vector DB, hosting) is billed to your own
account and typically runs Rs 15,000 - Rs 1,20,000 per month at our clients'
volumes.

6. WHAT IS INCLUDED IN THE SPRINT PRICE?
Feature-selection report, a working prototype, production-quality code
merged into your repo, an evaluation suite with a pass/fail gate, a
one-page cost/latency model, and 2 weeks of post-launch support. Not
included: third-party API usage, cloud costs, and any redesign of
surrounding product surfaces.

7. DO WE NEED AN AI OR ML TEAM TO WORK WITH YOU?
No. You need one technical owner (an engineer who can review PRs) and one
product decision-maker. We deliberately build so your existing engineers
can maintain the feature after handover, and we spend the last week
pairing with them.

8. WHO OWNS THE CODE AND THE IP?
You do. All code, prompts, evaluation sets and documentation created in the
engagement are assigned to you on final payment. We retain no licence and
do not reuse your data or domain logic in other engagements.

9. CAN WE USE OUR OWN STACK AND CLOUD?
Yes. We build on your stack by default - TypeScript/Python, your existing
API, your AWS/GCP/Azure account, your auth and logging. If you have no
preference, our default is Next.js or FastAPI, Postgres, and a hosted
model provider, all in your cloud account.

10. WHO HOLDS THE LLM API KEYS?
You do. Keys live in your own provider account and your own server-side
environment variables. They are never committed, never placed in browser
code, and never printed in logs. We ask for a scoped, spend-capped key for
the build period and hand it back at the end.

11. IS OUR CUSTOMER DATA SAFE?
We work inside your infrastructure and contracts. We do not copy
production data to our machines; where realistic samples are needed, we use
de-identified extracts with your written approval. We can sign your NDA and
DPA, and we support data-residency requirements for Indian users. You remain
the data fiduciary under applicable Indian law; we act as your processor.

12. HOW DO YOU MAKE SURE THE AI FEATURE IS ACTUALLY GOOD?
Every Sprint ends with an evaluation suite that encodes your acceptance
criteria in code - accuracy, refusal behaviour, latency and cost per
request. The feature only counts as shipped if it passes that suite. We do
not tune the test to make a weak feature look green.

13. WHAT IF THE AI FEATURE DOESN'T WORK OUT?
If the chosen feature fails its acceptance criteria in the build window,
we tell you plainly, hand over all work and the evaluation evidence, and
either (a) run a reset sprint at 50% cost to change approach, or (b) refund
the final milestone. Discovery-only engagements carry no such risk - you
get the recommendation and the reasoning regardless.

14. HOW DO YOU HANDLE HALLUCINATIONS AND BAD OUTPUTS?
By containing them: retrieval grounded in your own content, structured
outputs with schema validation, confidence thresholds with a safe fallback
to the non-AI path, and human review where the action is irreversible. We
also log every input/output pair so you can debug real failures.

15. WILL THIS BE EXPENSIVE TO RUN AT SCALE?
We model it before you commit. The Sprint deliverable includes cost per
request, cost per active user per month, and the volume at which the
feature stops being economical. Clients typically launch under
Rs 40,000/month and stay under Rs 1,20,000/month at 10,000+ monthly
requests.

16. DO YOU WORK ON-SITE OR REMOTE?
Our team is in Bengaluru. Expect one on-site day per week (your office or
ours in Koramangala) and the rest remote, with a 30-minute standup three
days a week and a written Friday update.

17. CAN YOU WORK ALONGSIDE OUR EXISTING ENGINEERING TEAM?
Yes - that is the preferred model. We take the uncertain AI work (model
choice, prompts, retrieval, evaluation) and your team keeps the product
surface, design and surrounding systems. Shared repository, shared PR
review, no separate side channel.

18. WHAT HAPPENS AFTER THE SPRINT?
Two weeks of included support, then one of: your team takes it (with the
handover docs and pairing week), a light care retainer from Rs 60,000/month
for monitoring, evaluation upkeep and small improvements, or an ongoing
product retainer at Rs 3,50,000 - Rs 6,00,000/month for continued AI
shipping.

19. HOW DO PAYMENTS AND CONTRACTS WORK?
40% on signing, 40% at prototype acceptance, 20% on final handover. Invoices
in INR, GST extra, payable within 15 days. Monthly retainers are billed
in advance on a 1-month rolling term with 30 days' notice. Standard MSA,
NDA and DPA are available; we can start on your paper.

20. HOW DO WE GET STARTED?
Email hello@firstcut.example with a short description of your product, your
stage, and the user problem you suspect AI could solve. We reply within one
working day and, if it looks like a fit, hold a free 30-minute scoping call
within the week. A paid Discovery Sprint is usually the fastest way to a
decision.
